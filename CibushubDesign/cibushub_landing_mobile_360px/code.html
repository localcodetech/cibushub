Roots & Meaning

Why we built CibusHub

The name blends Cibus (the Latin root for pure sustenance, wholesome food, and shared table nourishment) with Hub (the gathering core of our Accra neighborhoods).

We built this platform because Accra's greatest food secrets aren't confined to multinational franchises—they live in the cast-iron pots of Aunties in Osu, roadside grill masters in Spintex, and generational chop bars in Madina.
