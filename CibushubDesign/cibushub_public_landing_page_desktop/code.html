Frequently asked questions

Honest answers about coverage, costs, and support across Accra.

Which areas in Accra do you deliver to?
We currently dispatch across East Legon, Osu, Cantonments, Airport Residential, Spintex, Dzorwulu, Madina, and Labone. Riders navigate using both verified GPS coordinates and customary landmark notes.
How are delivery fees calculated?
We charge a transparent base fee of GHS 8.00 for trips up to 2 km, plus GHS 3.50 per additional kilometre. The total delivery price is locked and visible before you authorize your MoMo payment.
Can I pay with MTN Mobile Money or Telecel Cash?
Yes. We support all major Ghanaian network wallets including MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, and AT Money, as well as debit cards processed via Bank of Ghana approved rails on Paystack.
What happens if a restaurant runs out of food?
If a kitchen informs us that a pot has finished, the order is cancelled immediately and an instant automatic refund is sent directly back to your initiating MoMo wallet or card account.
How do I reach customer support?
You can access live support through the active order screen in-app or contact our Accra dispatch operations center directly on +233 30 200 4567 between 07:00 and 23:00 every day.
