Help Centre

Frequently asked questions

We operate actively across East Legon, Osu, Spintex, Airport Residential, Dzorwulu, Cantonments, Labone, Madina, and Adenta. New coverage zones are added monthly.
Delivery starts at a flat base of GHS 8.00 for the first 2 kilometres, plus GHS 2.50 per additional kilometre directly to your gate. There are never any weather surge surprises.
When you tap checkout, an instant USSD prompt appears on your phone screen asking for your MoMo PIN. Once approved, your order goes straight onto the kitchen pot board.
If your chosen protein or soup has been depleted, the order is cancelled in the app and your funds reverse back to your mobile money account automatically within seconds.
You can reach our Accra direct dispatch line every day from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM at +233 (0) 30 200 4820 or via WhatsApp directly inside the tracker screen.
