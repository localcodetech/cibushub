How CibusHub works

Three transparent steps designed around how Accra cooks, pays, and navigates.

1

Choose your food

Browse real menus from nearby kitchens and chop bars. Pick your spice level, proteins, and extras like extra shito, fried plantains, or boiled eggs.

2

Pay safely with MoMo or card

Direct checkout via Paystack with MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, AT Money, or bank card. No extra platform hidden markups or foreign exchange friction.

3

Track your rider live

Follow your courier along Accra roads directly to your landmark. Share your 4-digit Handshake PIN at your gate for safe release of your warm package.

Heritage & Purpose

Our Story

CibusHub was built to solve the real everyday friction of food delivery in Accra — where addresses are landmarks, kitchens cook in daily cauldrons, and mobile money is the heartbeat of commerce. The name Cibus comes from the Latin word for food and nourishment, paired with Hub for community connection.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you order, pay, and track with complete confidence.

We currently deliver across East Legon, Airport Residential, Osu, Cantonments, Dzorwulu, Roman Ridge, Spintex, and Madina, with new zones launching monthly.
Fees start at a base rate of GHS 8.00 for the first 2 km, plus GHS 2.50 per additional kilometre calculated directly to your gate.
We accept MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, AT Money, and local or international debit/credit cards through Paystack.
If a pot runs out or an order cannot be fulfilled, your payment is refunded immediately back to your original Mobile Money wallet or card.
